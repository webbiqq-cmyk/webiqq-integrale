The idea

Named for
the original.

Integrale takes its name from the cars that defined an era of rally heritage — precise, obsessive, built for people who care about the details. The coffee bar carries the same standard: a proper machine, a small tight menu, and a room that doubles as a showroom.

Come for the espresso. Stay for the Porsche parked by the window.
